# Qotom blocking IPC profile admission

This module admits only the fixed two-subject Qotom blocking-IPC profile after its earlier platform checkpoints, three distinct roots, exact root scans and entry gates, masked interrupt sources, two saved contexts, and generated-model bindings all agree. Its scalar results describe the one bounded trace and do not prove that hardware supplied the observations.

- `cpl3_authority_requires_admission` — If the profile reports CPL3 authority, every scalar platform, root, gate, interrupt, context-bank, and model-binding condition in the complete Qotom blocking-IPC admission predicate must have passed.
